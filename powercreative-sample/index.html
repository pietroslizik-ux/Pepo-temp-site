How we work

A clear path from the first chat to the cut.

Power Creative already publishes the steps. This page just puts them in an order a new client can follow.

  1. 1

    Tell us the brief

    Who the film is for, who is in it, dates that matter, the messages, and whether you want interviews, voiceover, cutaways or graphics. If a budget or a three-quote comparison is in play, say so early.

  2. 2

    A discovery chat

    Phone, Teams, Zoom or a meeting. The aim is a strategy plan and a sense of whether the fit is right.

  3. 3

    Build the right team

    Script writing, media direction, storyboarding, talent and crew. The team grows or shrinks with the strategy.

  4. 4

    Get creative

    Visual ideas, then the move from paper to film. That is where set, makeup, talent and direction come in.

  5. 5

    Shoot, then sit with the cut

    Production brings subject, talent and location together. Post is a run of draft previews, remote or in the room, until the piece is ready.
