Print options

Prefer paper? Download your planner, then upload the PDF to a print shop. Ask for A4 or US Letter at 100% scale, no scaling.

Budget: Officeworks

Upload the PDF to Officeworks Bound Documents and collect it free from any store.

We recommend: A4, wire binding, a Leathercraft back cover and 210gsm silk or parchment paper.

  • Coil binding: $4.50 to $9.50
  • Wire binding: $5.50 to $7
  • Comb binding: $3.25 to $7
  • Thermal binding: $3.25 to $4.75
  • Leathercraft back cover: $1.25
  • Silk or gloss paper: +24c to 30c
  • Parchment paper: +59c

Luxury: hardcover

For a keepsake planner with a hardcover, heavier paper, cloth or foil. All of these print your own PDF and take 1 copy.

  • ePrint Online hardcover notebooks (Brisbane): rigid hardcover, A5, A4 or custom, instant quote. Also a case-bound hardcover book on 100 or 120 gsm uncoated paper.
  • Mixam planners: casebound or Smyth-sewn hardcover on uncoated 90–200 gsm paper, or lay-flat wire. See also Mixam journals.
  • Inkwattle (Melbourne): foil-stamped cloth hardcover with dust jacket, ribbon and printed endpapers. Quote only, from one copy, ships to AU, NZ, UK and worldwide.

Tip: for a book-style hardcover, leave a 10–15 mm inner margin, because it won't open fully flat. Pick uncoated paper if you use highlighters or fountain pens.
