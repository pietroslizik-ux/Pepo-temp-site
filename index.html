A note from Peter

Hi. One little birdie told me some kid in the neighbourhood is scared of me. Sorry, very sorry to hear that. Even though I cannot affect how people see me and understand my quirkiness, and frankly, I don't even mind, I'd never do anything to frighten a child. Kids aren't born scared of people. They learn how to feel by watching the grown-ups around them, so how a child acts usually comes from what they've been shown at home. All I can do is be kind, keep to myself, and be a good neighbour, and that's what I'll keep doing. I'm sorry, not sorry, for my quirkiness. I've been hiding for long enough. I'm not sorry now.

— Peter

What Pepo Studios is

Pepo Studios is my project. Mr. Pepo is the guy with the light bulb: quirky, witty and full of ideas, because he is neurodiverse. I turn his ideas into practical printable paper products, colouring sheets and bedtime story books for kids, families and teachers.

Pepo Studios has applied for a Create NSW grant to bring Mr. Pepo and the Paper Sky to families.

For people like me, the overwhelm isn't random — it shows up when I'm in deep focus, whether that's work on the computer, exercise, or anything else. When I'm in that stage and someone rattles me, it can feel like slamming on the brakes. To others it can look constant, but it's tied to the focus.

The cause

It is a neurodiversity awareness project by Pepo Studios. Different brains build brilliant things, and every page you print helps more people see that. No big company, just a lot of paper, glue and ideas.

Why I'm doing this

I'm doing all this because I came to the realisation of what I've been suffering all my life, and how little awareness there is about that around us.

Most of the people with this kind of ability, as I like to say, not disability, are suffering greatly, and lots of them I see are called the scum of the earth now. And lots of them are the masters who control the earth.

Because these minds can either be super incredible and do super things, or can very easily wind down on a bad path. So that's what I would like to point out.

— Peter

Moving forward

I'm fed up with how the system treats people like me. But I'm not bitter. I'm turning that anger into strength.

I'm not asking for pity, and I'm not desperate. I'm pumped, and I'm pushing forward for the right cause.

I want this to reach as many people as possible, here in Australia and all around the world. Free for everyone, or support what it's worth.

— Peter

Alpha Mastermind

Alpha Mastermind is a small group for people with a special ability who are building something real. Approved members get a private page and an access code.

Look and think before you speak.

I'm starting a mastermind for people with special abilities. Minds that notice things most people walk right past. It's not about being better than anyone. It's about finally having room to think out loud with people who get it. If that sounds like you, keep an eye on this page.

Limited to 20 members per class.

How to get in

Alpha Mastermind is for people with a special ability who are building something real, have started or tried things, and want to help others too. If that's you, apply.

  1. Apply with your name, email, and your special ability or what you want to build.
  2. Answer a few questions.
  3. Peter reviews every application personally.
  4. Approved members get the private page and access code by email.

Inside, approved members have a private discussion area for business ideas. It is visible only to people with the access code.

Other groups exist or can be created. Each one is looked after by a designated host, so everyone who fits has a place.

Hosts can run their own groups under Alpha Mastermind supervision. It's joint supervision, so many people have input, not just one person. As the owner, I want to hear what's working and what isn't. Tell me straight. I don't want to be misinformed.

Weekly sessions

I'd like to start one-hour sessions once a week. We'll pick the day that suits the group, Tuesday, Wednesday or Thursday. I don't mind which. I'm very happy to talk through plans for any future business, the pros and cons, and how to make them happen. I won't spend any time on why something can't be done. I only focus on how it can be done.

Alpha Mastermind is full (20 members). Apply anyway and Peter will place you in another group or the next opening.

Free Halloween window ghost

A friendly floating ghost for your window. Free to download and print at home.

  1. Download the ghost PDF.
  2. Take a plain A4 sheet and put three straight stripes of double-sided tape on it.
  3. Stick a big sheet of baking paper onto the taped A4.
  4. Trim the edges to A4 with scissors, or with a guillotine if you have one (a guillotine is easier).
  5. Print the ghost onto the baking paper side.
  6. Put it on the window.
