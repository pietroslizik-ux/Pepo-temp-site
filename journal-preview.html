A little paper idea

  1. One thing I noticed

    The kitchen light landed on a scrap of yellow paper. It looked a little like a tiny window.

  2. One thought I want to share

    A small paper house can begin with one leftover piece. I do not need a whole new sheet to start. Starting small feels kinder than waiting for a perfect plan.

  3. One small thing I'm making or trying

    I folded one corner, then another. The scrap stood up on its own. Next I might try a little door, just to see if it opens. The fold marks made a soft shadow, and that was enough for today.
